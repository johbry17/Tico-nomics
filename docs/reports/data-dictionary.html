Data

Sources, variables, coverage, and definitions

This study examines World Bank development finance in Costa Rica from 1980 through 2025. It combines project records, loan-level financial data, private-sector investment disclosures, political risk guarantees, and national development indicators. The sources differ in coverage, reporting dates, and financial definitions; their values are not directly interchangeable.

Original files are stored in data/raw/. Source preparation and reconciliation are handled by scripts in src/; processed datasets are stored in data/processed/ and data/derived/.

Financial measures

IBRD loan records distinguish approved commitments from subsequent financial activity.

Variable Definition
Original principal Board-approved loan amount, nominal USD
Cancelled amount Principal cancelled after approval
Net principal Original principal less cancellations, floored at zero
Disbursed amount Cumulative loan drawdowns reported at the source snapshot
Repaid to IBRD Cumulative repayments
Loans held Net IBRD exposure reported at the snapshot

Original principal is assigned to the loan’s Board Approval Year. Disbursements and repayments are cumulative snapshot values, not annual flows.

IBRD guarantees are excluded from the loan analysis. No guarantees were identified in the Costa Rica loan records.

The Gini series is survey-based and has gaps. The 2025 observation is preliminary. Life expectancy data extend through 2024.

Source-specific qualifications

Project dates. The Projects & Operations catalogue supplies canonical project approval dates. These were checked against API V3 date-recovery records. The IBRD Statement’s Board Approval Date is used separately to assign approval years to loan-level financial records.

IBRD coverage. The API V2 extract does not contain every project appearing in the catalogue or loan statement. The $420 million Health Insurance project (P148435), approved in 2016, is present in the loan statement and project catalogue but absent from the V2 extract. The loan statement is authoritative for the financial analysis.

IFC investments. Amounts represent IFC own-account investment, not total project financing or syndicated funding. Most investment records have projected Board dates; dates and period assignments should therefore be treated as indicative. The pending Insulet project is excluded from the historical study period.

MIGA guarantees. Fiscal-year labels are retained as published. Gross exposure represents maximum guarantee liability. Repeated disclosures for the Papagayo tourism project are not additive; the FY2003 record of $81 million is retained.

Inflation adjustment. The U.S. GDP deflator adjusts USD commitments for changes in U.S. prices. It does not measure Costa Rican purchasing power or local inflation. The 2025 deflator observation may be revised.

Scope and exclusions

The primary financial analysis covers IBRD sovereign lending approved from 1980 through 2025. IFC investments and MIGA guarantees are presented separately because their financial measures represent different forms of development finance.

The study does not cover bilateral assistance, IMF or IDB financing, domestic borrowing, or private investment beyond the disclosed IFC and MIGA records.
