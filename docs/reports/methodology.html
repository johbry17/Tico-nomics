Methods

Research design, processing, and analytical decisions

Research Design

This study examines the scale, composition, and evolution of World Bank Group development finance in Costa Rica from 1980 through 2025. The primary analysis is descriptive, centered on IBRD sovereign lending. IFC investments and MIGA guarantees provide separate perspectives on private-sector finance and political risk coverage.

The analysis identifies changes in financing patterns and places them alongside national development indicators. It does not estimate the causal effects of World Bank financing on Costa Rica’s economic or social outcomes.

Project Reconciliation

The World Bank Projects & Operations catalogue provides the project universe and canonical project approval dates. IBRD loan records are matched to projects by project ID. API V2 records supplement project metadata, sector allocations, and abstracts; they are not treated as a complete project inventory.

API V3 records were used to corroborate missing approval dates in V2. Where dates could not be recovered, they remain missing rather than being inferred.

Loan-level financial records are aggregated by project ID before joining project metadata. This prevents multiple loans associated with one project from duplicating project-level records or financial totals.

IBRD Financial Analysis

The analysis filters the IBRD Statement to Costa Rica loan records and excludes guarantees. Original principal and cancellations are retained separately.

Net principal is calculated as:

\[ \text{Net principal} = \max(\text{Original principal} - \text{Cancellations}, 0) \]

The zero floor prevents terminated records with no original principal from producing negative net commitments. Projects with fully cancelled loans remain in project counts but contribute no net principal. The undisbursed COVID-19 Vaccines project (P178320) is retained in project counts with zero original principal.

Annual commitments are assigned to the Board Approval Year recorded in the loan statement. Years without approvals are retained as zero in the annual series.

Inflation Adjustment

Nominal IBRD commitments are converted to approximate 2025 dollars using the U.S. GDP deflator:

\[ \text{Real amount}_{2025} = \text{Nominal amount}_{t} \times \frac{\text{Deflator}_{2025}}{\text{Deflator}_{t}} \]

The same adjustment is applied to net principal. This provides a consistent U.S.-price adjustment for USD-denominated amounts; it is not a measure of Costa Rican inflation or purchasing power.

Instrument Classification

Projects are classified as Investment Project Financing (IPF) or Development Policy Financing (DPL). Classification uses loan-type fields and project-name patterns, followed by a manual override file for reviewed exceptions.

The resulting categories are analytical classifications. They are not necessarily the formal instrument labels used at the time of approval. Automatic classifications, overrides, and override reasons are retained in the classification review output.

Sector Analysis

Project sector allocations are drawn from API metadata, with project catalogue metadata used where necessary. Projects may have multiple sectors. Principal is allocated across reported sectors using their stated percentage shares:

\[ \text{Allocated principal} = \text{Project principal} \times \frac{\text{Sector percentage}}{100} \]

Reported percentages are not renormalized when they do not sum to 100%. Projects without usable percentage allocations do not contribute to sector-level dollar totals. Sector results therefore describe the subset of lending with usable allocation metadata, not the full portfolio.

Granular sector labels are consolidated into broader analytical groups for comparison across periods.

Project Abstracts

Project abstracts are analyzed using six manually defined keyword groups: Infrastructure, Environment/Climate, Social Development, Governance/Fiscal, Competitiveness/Trade, and Disaster Risk.

The analysis measures keyword presence, not project outcomes or semantic themes. Period percentages use projects with available abstracts as the denominator. Periods without abstracts are treated as missing, not as zero.

Word frequencies are calculated after removing stop words and selected generic terms. These results are exploratory and sensitive to abstract availability and wording.

IFC and MIGA

IFC disclosures are grouped by project number to avoid counting multiple disclosure documents as separate projects. Approved own-account amounts are extracted from the disclosure text. Actual approval dates are used where available; otherwise, projected Board dates provide indicative period assignments. Pipeline projects are excluded from the historical investment analysis.

MIGA disclosures are deduplicated by project. Repeated disclosures for the Papagayo project represent successive exposure records, not additive guarantees. The FY2003 record of $81 million is retained as the reported maximum exposure.

Development Context

World Development Indicators are filtered to Costa Rica and the study period. Missing observations are retained; no interpolation is performed. The U.S. GDP deflator is applied only to IBRD financial amounts, not to the national development indicators.

Reproducibility and Validation

The processing pipeline is organized as follows:

Script Function
00_setup.R Dependencies, paths, and study-period settings
01_import_sources.R Source imports and inventory
02_prepare_wdi.R Development indicators and deflator
03_reconcile_projects.R Project universe and date reconciliation
04_prepare_ibrd.R IBRD loan preparation and aggregation
05_prepare_ifc.R IFC disclosure preparation
06_prepare_miga.R MIGA disclosure preparation
07_analysis.R Analytical datasets and summaries
08_visualizations.R Figures
09_validation.R Structural and selected numerical checks

Shared formatting, classification, and export functions are maintained in functions.R. Validation checks cover key record counts, financial totals, selected project treatments, and figure outputs. They are targeted checks, not a complete test of every derived value or visualization.
